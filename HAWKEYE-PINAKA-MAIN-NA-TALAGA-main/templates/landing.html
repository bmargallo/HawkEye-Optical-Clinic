Questions patients ask.

Is my information private?

Accounts are email-verified, passwords can be reset by email and every form is protected against forged requests. Only you and your clinic team can see your record.

What if I can’t make my appointment?

Send a new request from your portal or message the clinic, and staff will confirm another time. You can also dismiss the notice. Your appointment history stays on record.

Do I need an account?

An account lets you view your prescription, appointments and reminders and message the clinic. Sign up and confirm your email address. The clinic confirms every visit: send a request in the portal, walk in, or call.

Does the portal give medical advice?

No. It supports clinic operations only. It does not diagnose, prescribe or check clinical accuracy. Your eye care professional does that at your visit.
